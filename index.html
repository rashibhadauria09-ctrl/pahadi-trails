THE MOUNTAIN RULE

Go prepared.
Come back safe.

Altitude, cold, rain and long descents can turn a beautiful day into a difficult one. Build your fitness gradually, keep a flexible schedule and never push on just to reach a summit.

01 / FITNESS

Train before the climb

Start with brisk walking, stairs and light leg-strength exercises several weeks ahead. Increase distance gradually and include rest days.

02 / LAYERS

Pack for changing weather

Use a moisture-wicking base layer, warm mid-layer and wind/rain shell. Add a hat, gloves and spare socks in cold conditions.

03 / ALTITUDE

Listen to your body

Headache, nausea, dizziness and unusual fatigue can be signs of altitude illness. Stop ascending and seek help if symptoms appear or worsen.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY

Respect the mountain

Stay on marked trails, follow local instructions, avoid loud music and fires, and never disturb wildlife or remove plants.
