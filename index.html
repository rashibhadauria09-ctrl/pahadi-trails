LEAVE ONLY FOOTPRINTS

Travel well.
Travel lightly.

Small choices make a difference to the mountains, the local community and your own experience.

01

Pack in layers

Carry warm clothes, rain protection, sturdy footwear and a reusable bottle.

02

Respect the trail

Take your waste back and avoid disturbing wildlife or damaging plants.

03

Check conditions

Check weather, road access, permits and official advice before travelling.

04

Support local

Choose local guides and homestays when possible, and respect local customs.
